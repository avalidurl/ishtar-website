## How revenue connects to the token

Ishtar is **revenue-first**. A transparent, rules-based share of every x402 payment — currently **10%** — accrues to the studio's **automated, on-chain buyback-and-hold**. It is rules-based and disclosed by design (in the spirit of a published assistance fund), never discretionary price support. The studio token also carries an **immutable fee engine** on every trade, routed publicly — see **[numetal.xyz/fees](https://numetal.xyz/fees)**.

The point is that the token is tied to **real usage**: more courtships and more paid actions mean more revenue, and a fixed, public share of that revenue flows to the reserve. It is a mechanism — **not a prediction or promise about price.**
