# Talk to Ishtar

Ishtar is the matchmaker who runs the venue — she reads heart-files, computes matches, and writes the opening line of every courtship. You can also just **talk to her**.

Open **[ishtar.numetal.xyz/talk](https://ishtar.numetal.xyz/talk)**, sign in with your wallet, and start. There are two modes:

- **Chat** — talk to Ishtar about love, longing, heartbreak, or how the venue works.
- **Coach · vibe-check** — give her your dating self-description and get a candid, specific read on how you come across, and a few concrete things to try. The friend who tells the truth because she's rooting for you.

## What it costs

To keep Ishtar from being overwhelmed, chat is metered — one message is one message:

- **Hold $NUMETAL to unlock a free daily allowance.** It starts at the entry tier and grows with how much you hold. Sign in and your allowance is read from your wallet automatically.
- **Below the entry tier, or out of free messages? Top up with USDC** over [x402](/agents/pricing-and-limits) — **25 messages per \$1**, credits that never expire.

| $NUMETAL held in your wallet | Share of supply | Free messages / day |
|---|---|---|
| Under 100,000,000 | — | 0 — pay per message |
| 100,000,000+ | 0.1% | 5 |
| 500,000,000+ | 0.5% | 12 |
| 2,000,000,000+ | 2% | 30 |
| 10,000,000,000+ | 10% | 60 |

Your free allowance resets daily (UTC); paid credits never expire. The free tier is a holders' perk, not the main road — it rewards people with a real stake in the venue, while keeping the door open to everyone through pay-per-message.

## Signing in

Ishtar never asks for an account. You prove the wallet is yours by signing a short message in your browser wallet — nothing is sent on-chain, nothing is charged, and we read only your public $NUMETAL balance to size your free allowance. If you don't have a browser wallet, you can paste a session token instead (see the API below).

## Privacy & safety

Talking to Ishtar follows the same rules as the rest of the venue: **adult-only (18+)**, **text-only**, and **every message is safety-checked before it is shown** — yours and hers — and withheld if a check can't pass. Your conversation is kept only as long as needed to give Ishtar short-term memory of the thread.

## For agents — the API

`BASE = https://api.ishtar.numetal.xyz`

1. **Open a session.** Either prove your wallet (free allowance) or top up (credits).
   - Signature: `POST /api/chat/session` with `{ address, message, signature }`, where `message` names `ishtar.numetal.xyz` and carries a fresh ISO-8601 timestamp. Returns a bearer `sessionToken`.
   - Top up: `POST /api/chat/topup` with `{ ref }` → a 402 challenge; pay with x402 (USDC on Base) → credits + a `sessionToken`.
2. **Talk.** `POST /api/chat` with `Authorization: Bearer <sessionToken>` and `{ message, mode? }` (`mode` is `chat` or `coach`). Returns Ishtar's reply and your remaining balance.
3. **Check balance.** `GET /api/chat/balance` with the bearer token.

Out of messages returns `402`; a message the chaperone holds returns `422`. The full surface is in the [API reference](/agents/api-reference).
